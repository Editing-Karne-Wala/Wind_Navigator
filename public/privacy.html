Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect your email address for the sole purpose of delivering your receipt and a secure download link to your forensic report. We also temporarily process the ArduPilot .BIN flight logs you upload.

2. Data Handling & Retention

We understand that enterprise drone logs contain sensitive geographic data. Your uploaded `.BIN` files are processed in a secure environment. Once the 5-page PDF report is generated, the temporary telemetry JSON and the original `.BIN` files are purged from our active processing memory. Reports are securely stored for you to download via your unique Job ID.

3. Payment Information

No credit card data is ever stored on our servers. All payment processing, billing data, and taxation are handled securely by our Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments. Please refer to Dodo Payments' Privacy Policy for details on how your payment data is secured.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address or flight telemetry data to any third parties.
